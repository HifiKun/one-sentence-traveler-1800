An entry for [make.fun's "One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb).

The task: write one sentence of at most 200 characters explaining the modern
internet to someone arriving from the year 1800 — no words coined after their
time, no list of features, just a picture they could actually hold in their
head. The program writes that sentence to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The sentence

> Imagine one road joining every post office, library, and market on earth, so any letter, book, or spoken word reaches anyone, anywhere, faster than a breath.

157 characters. It is built only from things a person from 1800 already
knows — a road, a post office, a library, a market — and each one maps to
something the internet really does: the road is the connection itself, the
post office is messages, the library is knowledge, the market is trade, and
"faster than a breath" is how quickly a letter, a book, or a spoken word
travels it to anyone, anywhere. One image, nothing crammed in, and no word
in it was coined after 1800.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` checks the sentence against the 200-character limit, then writes
it to `/output/entry.txt`.
